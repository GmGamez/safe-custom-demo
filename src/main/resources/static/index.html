AI-assisted Support QA review

Every closed Zendesk ticket runs through deterministic checks and three parallel AI-agent evaluations against Safe Software's shared 9-category QA rubric, with a configurable selection engine routing the tickets that most need a human look to a Team Lead. AI-generated assessments are provisional — the Team Lead remains the owner of every final QA decision.

The rubric: Communication, Technical Advice, and Process Adherence — three categories each, scored 4-Excellent / 3-Meets Standard / 2-Developing / 1-Does Not Meet, or Not Applicable. Categories are weighted (Technical Advice counts most, at 44.4% of the final score) to produce one overall percentage and rating: Exceeds, Meets, or Needs Improvement.
